Owner, second generation

My father started with one kadhai

In 1987, my father, Late Shri Ram Prakash Gupta, opened this shop with a single kadhai and a borrowed table. I remember sitting on a sack of sugar at age eight, watching him turn jalebis before sunrise.

I took over in 2008 and my younger brother Mukesh now runs the frying counter. We still use his recipes. Only the weighing scale has changed.

When you buy from us, you are buying what we eat at home.

  • Pure desi ghee in every mithai
  • Made fresh daily, nothing carried over
  • Sugar levels adjusted on request
  • Honest weight, we weigh in front of you
  • Same family behind the counter for 37 years
